ProxyPilot investigates how remote collaborators can delegate and reclaim control of a telepresence robot while maintaining agency during interaction.

## Research motivation

Remote users must often balance conversation with the demands of operating a robot. Rather than treating manual control and autonomy as fixed alternatives, this project explores transitions between them.

## System and interaction design

ProxyPilot uses an AI-assisted telepresence system to investigate delegation of navigation, viewpoint adjustment and gesture. The research examines how users authorize, monitor and reclaim delegated actions during collaboration.
