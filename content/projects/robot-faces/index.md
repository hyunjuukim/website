*Research in progress*

This project revisits a foundational study of rendered robot faces through a replication with 68 new participants. It examines how visual design choices shape perceptions of robot faces.

## Research approach

We replicate the evaluation of static rendered faces and extend the research toward animated expressive cues, including blinking, gaze and idle behavior. The project develops open-source face assets and a reusable web-based evaluation framework.

## Status

The replication manuscript is under review at HRI 2027 Short Contributions. The animated-face extension is ongoing.
